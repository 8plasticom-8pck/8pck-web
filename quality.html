Sustainability

rPET and the EU Packaging Rules

PPWR compliance is a document question, not a marketing one. We supply the declaration per order.

PCR and rPET resin are available on request, with the recycled content stated on the declaration. For food-contact applications we confirm the grade before quoting.

Where a customer needs mono-material packaging for recyclability, we quote PET body with PET closure rather than PP, and say clearly what it costs.
