Who Invoices You, and Why It Is Not the Same Company as This Website

8PCK is the export desk of CXWN, the factory in Shantou. The contract, the invoice and the payment go to the factory. This page explains the arrangement plainly, because buyers deserve to know before they wire money.

The Arrangement, in Four Lines

Website and enquiries8PCK — the English-facing desk. Joe answers the email.
Contract and invoiceShantou Chuangxin Weinuo Plastic Packaging Products Co., Ltd. (CXWN)
PaymentTo CXWN's company account. The account name matches the contract, not the website.
Production and after-salesCXWN's workshop and QC; 8PCK handles the correspondence in English.

Why It Is Set up This Way

A factory in Haojiang does not want to run an English website, and a website does not have a mould shop. Splitting the two lets one person answer in your language while the work happens under one roof — instead of passing your file through a trading company that adds a margin and a delay.

What it is not: a reseller with an unknown supplier behind it. The factory is named, its registration number is published, and its certificates are on file with numbers you can check.

How to Verify It Before You Pay

  1. Check the company name on the contract against the invoice and the bank account.
  2. Look up the unified social credit code (91440512576471585D) on gsxt.gov.cn.
  3. Ask for the ISO 9001 certificate number (PCC85724Q01277R1S) and check it with the issuing body.
  4. Ask for a video call in the workshop. We do these weekly, and the film on the factory page is the same floor.

If any of those four does not line up, do not send money — tell us instead.

What 8PCK Is For

It exists so an English-speaking buyer can find the factory, read the specification in English, and get an answer without a middleman. The commercial relationship is with CXWN; the conversation is with us.
