Custom development

Copy an Existing Bottle, or Start From a Drawing

Two very different paths. Either we match your pack against 3,000+ existing moulds — no tooling, 1,000 pcs per colour — or you commission a new mould, which is a capital cost: from USD 850 for one simple blow-moulded bottle, and USD 16,000–27,500 for a four-part assembly. Custom tooling only pays back on repeat volume; below a few thousand pieces a year the stock route is almost always the right answer.

These are per-mould figures, not project totals. A new pack usually needs more than one mould, plus trial samples and often a second cavity — budget the project, not the line item. Mould ownership and exclusivity are agreed in writing before the first payment.

Before You Commission a Mould

A mould is a capital cost that sits in front of the unit price. It only makes sense when the volume is there to absorb it — so we say this early rather than after a drawing review.

Annual volumeWhat we recommendTooling, in our shop
Under 3,000 pcsStock mould, or a stock shape with decorationNo tooling; 1,000 pcs per colour
3,000–10,000 pcsOne cavity on a new shape, if the design truly needs itUSD 850–9,500 per mould; 15–40 days
10,000–30,000 pcsTwo cavities; consider a second colourAbout 1.6× a single-cavity mould
Over 30,000 pcsMulti-cavity, dedicated line timeAbout 2.6× and up; needs a volume commitment

These are ranges from our own recent orders, not an industry standard — another factory may quote half or double, and both can be honest. A four-part assembly changes the arithmetic: USD 16,000–27,500 of tooling is normal, and it does not shrink because the first order is small. If the launch is a test, start on a stock pack and move to the custom one when the volume is proven — that is the lower in unit cost path, and we will say so.
