    How it is calculated

    The Formula, the Assumptions, and Where It Stops

    1. Neck range per capacity: 18/410 up to 40 ml, 20/410 up to 120 ml, 24/410 up to 600 ml, 28/410 up to 800 ml.
    2. Wall floor: 0.45 mm general, 0.55 mm with a pump, 0.65 mm above 600 ml.
    3. Closure compatibility is checked against the standard closure range for that neck.

    Assumptions. Standard closure families; the real fit is confirmed on a filled sample.

    Standards we work to. ISO 9001:2015 (quality system, cert PCC85724Q01277R1S) · EU 10/2011 (plastic food-contact, migration report E2032) · RoHS (PE B-R01612E2033, PET B-R201133464) · ISO 4180 (packaging for transport). The calculator is a planning tool: the approved drawing and the migration report decide the final specification.

    Where it does not apply. It does not replace a drawing check of the thread and the sealing land.

    What the Checker Tests

    Neck and closure fit, capacity per neck, and wall thickness against the pump stroke.
