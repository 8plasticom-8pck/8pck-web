    About This Tool

    What does this checker actually test?

    Three things that cause most first-order failures: whether the neck fits the closure, whether the capacity suits that neck, and whether the wall is thick enough for a pump stroke.

    Why does wall thickness matter?

    A 500 ml bottle at 0.45 mm flexes under a pump stroke and the pump starts drawing air. 0.55 mm is the practical floor; 0.65 mm where the pump is large or the bottle is 750 ml.

    Can you tell me the exact price?

    No. The band is indicative for a first conversation; real price depends on weight, decoration and packing, and comes in a written quote within 24 hours.

    Is this the same as a 3D drawing check?

    No. This checks the specification logic; the drawing check (necks, threads, tolerances) happens on the actual file before tooling.
