    Why Headspace Matters More Than the Label Weight

    A jar labelled 240 ml holds 240 ml of product plus the air the filler leaves. With wax at 12 % headspace a 250 g pour needs a 300 ml jar, not a 240 ml one — which is why fill weight is not the same as capacity.

    How to Read the Result

    The number is the smallest stock size that holds the pour with headspace. If it falls between two sizes, take the larger one: an under-filled jar looks short on shelf and over-filled wax spills in transit.

    Questions About This Tool

    Is this a filling specification?

    No — it is a sizing check. The filler's own headspace requirement wins.

    What density should I use?

    Approximate: water 1.00, lotion 0.95, oil 0.90, candle wax 0.86, salt scrub 1.30.

    Can you make a custom size?
