How to Read It

  • Ranges come from our own quotations for the 3,000+ moulds CXWN runs, not from an industry index.
  • One cavity unless stated. Two cavities multiply the tool by about 1.6; four by about 2.6.
  • An unscrewing thread adds 30–45 % to that part, because the tool needs a rotating mechanism.
  • MOQ is per colour. It is set by the least flexible step: the mould if the shape is new, the decoration line if the part is sprayed.
  • Lead time excludes Chinese New Year (two to four weeks), and excludes your own sample-approval time.

What Moves a Band

Part count moves it most: each part is its own tool, trial and tolerance stack. Then the thread — a thread that strips off the core is cheap, one that must unscrew adds a mechanism. Then finish: a mirror polish on a clear part is hand work and cannot be scheduled like machining. Cavities come last, and rarely pay back under 20,000 pieces a year.

What This Page Is Not

It is not a quotation. Two bottles with the same volume can differ by a factor of three depending on the closure, the wall and the draw. The band tells you whether a project is in scope; the drawing decides the number.
