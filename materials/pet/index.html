Material

PET

Clear, light, food-grade, recyclable. The default for anything the customer must see.

What It Is Good At

Clarity, weight, cost. A 500 ml bottle at 20–28 g; the standard stream recycles it.

Where It Stops

Heat above about 60 °C, strong solvents, and a thick-wall premium look — PET goes cloudy when thick.

Typical Use

Shampoo, water, juice, toner, sanitizer.

Next Step

Tell us the formula's pH and solvent and we will confirm whether PET is the right call — or send a photo of the pack you are matching.

Choosing Between Two Resins?

Send the product and the target look; we answer with one recommendation, not a list.

Questions

Is PET food-safe?

Food-grade PET with the EU 10/2011 declaration is; ask for the grade when you specify.

Can PET be hot-filled?

Not above about 60 °C — it distorts, and the neck goes out of tolerance.
