How to Verify 8PCK Before You Send a Deposit

A website is not evidence. This page lists what can be checked independently, with the numbers to check — and what we do not claim.

Run the credit code through China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn). The record should match the legal name and address above. If it does not, stop.

Email ProCision or the laboratory with the reference number — both will confirm whether the certificate exists and what it covers. A supplier who cannot give you a number you can query is showing you a logo, not a certificate.

3. Check the Factory

Ask for a live video call and walk the mould shop, the blow moulding hall and the warehouse. Ours is at B03-05 Plot, Tatou, Brownstone Street, Haojiang District, Shantou, Guangdong, China; we do these calls on working days, GMT+8, and you can pick the rooms.

Then order a sample from the production mould — not a showroom piece — and weigh it. A bottle that is 2 g under spec is a bottle that fails on your filling line.

4. Check the Payment Terms

We do not ask for 100% of a first order by bank transfer. Standard terms are a deposit with the balance against the bill of lading, and a third-party inspection before shipment if you want one. If any supplier insists on full prepayment to a personal account, treat it as a signal.

5. What We Do Not Have (Yet)

  • No Alibaba Trade Assurance store — we trade direct, by email and contract.
  • No Trustpilot or review profile — our references are customer names you can call, given with permission.
  • No FDA, REACH SVHC or GRS certificate. We will say so rather than show a badge we cannot back.

That list is short on purpose. Everything else on this site should be traceable to a document or a number.
