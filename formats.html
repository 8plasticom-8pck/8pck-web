How it works

Ordering Across Formats

A bottle and a tube for the same brand can ship in one container. We quote them together and pack them together, which is usually cheaper than two suppliers and two shipments.

The constraint is decoration: tubes print on the body before filling, lipstick cases are assembled after coating. Both add 5–7 days, and both are quoted per artwork.
