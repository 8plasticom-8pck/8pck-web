What Buyers Get Wrong

Assuming the Supplier Covers the Drug

We supply the container. The licence holder qualifies the product; we provide the specification and resin declarations.

CR Cap Tested on a Different Bottle

A push-and-turn cap that passes on one 38-400 bottle can fail on another. Test the assembly.

Light Protection by Label

If the claim depends on light protection, specify amber resin at mould stage, not a label.

What We Supply, and What We Do Not

We mould and supply the container: HDPE or PET bottles, child-resistant closures, induction seal liners, and the carton if needed. We do not fill, we do not hold a drug manufacturing licence, and we do not issue pharmacopoeia compliance for the finished product.

That boundary matters for pharma buyers. What we can give is a packaging specification, resin declarations, and dimensional consistency batch to batch — the bottle side of the qualification.

Child-Resistant Closures

A push-and-turn CR cap is a separate moulded part and the most expensive component on a small pharma bottle. It has to be tested with the specific bottle neck: a cap that passes on one 38-400 bottle can fail on another with a different thread depth.

We test the assembled pack, not the cap alone, and keep the specification on file for reorders so a batch two years later uses the same tolerance.

Colour and Light Protection

Amber HDPE blocks part of the UV range; opaque white blocks more but reads clinical. For light-sensitive liquids, amber is the usual compromise. If the label claim depends on light protection, specify it at the mould stage, not with a label.

Frequently Asked

Are your bottles suitable for pharmaceutical use?

We supply the container to a defined specification. The drug product's qualification, pharmacopoeia compliance and filling are the licence holder's responsibility, not ours.

Do you supply child-resistant caps?

Yes — push-and-turn CR caps, tested on the specific bottle rather than supplied loose.

Can you supply induction seal liners?

Yes, where the bottle rim has a flat land wide enough for the seal. We check the rim before quoting.

What sizes are available?

30-500 ml in HDPE and PET, with 38-400 the most common neck for pill and supplement bottles.
