A Refill Duo That Shares One Closure Family

A 500 ml refill and a 100 ml travel companion. The engineering goal was that the same pump fits both, so the customer stocks one closure.

ItemSpecification
Capacity500 ml + 100 ml
MaterialPET, clear
Neck24/410 on both
ClosuresOne lotion pump family, two dip-tube lengths
Tooling2 moulds, 24 days

One Neck, Two Bottles

Fixing both bottles on 24/410 meant one closure purchase, one torque setting and one spare part. The cost was the 100 ml body: a 24/410 neck at that volume looks large, so the shoulder was raised to keep the proportion.

The Dip Tube Is the Only Difference

Same pump, two tube lengths. That is a stocking decision as much as an engineering one: the customer holds one pump SKU and two tubes instead of two complete closure SKUs.

What Went Wrong Once

The 100 ml tube was initially cut for the 500 ml bottle and touched the base, which made the pump feel stiff. Trimming 6 mm fixed it. In a two-size range, the tube length is the part that gets copied by mistake.

Questions

Can two sizes share one pump?

Yes, if both use the same neck — only the dip-tube length changes.

What goes wrong in a range?

The tube length gets copied between sizes and touches the base, which makes the pump feel stiff.
