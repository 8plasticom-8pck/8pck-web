Before you write

Four Questions We Get Every Week

What is your MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour on existing moulds. Below that we can quote stock colours.

Is there a tooling cost?

No on 3,000+ existing moulds. A new mould starts at USD 800 and takes 15–20 days.

Can I get a sample?

Yes. Existing samples are free, freight collect, dispatched in 7 days.

Which documents can you send?

ISO 9001:2015 certificate, RoHS verification of compliance, and the EU 10/2011 + 1935/2004 migration test report. Drawings and declarations go out with the quotation.
