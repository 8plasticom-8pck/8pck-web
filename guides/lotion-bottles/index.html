Lotion Bottles: Why 300 ml Is the Size Where the Pump Decides the Feel

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 2026

Body lotion is judged twice: on shelf by the bottle, in the hand by the pump. At 300 ml the two are almost the same decision — the wall thickness and the pump output together decide whether it feels cheap.

Body lotion sells in 200, 250, 300 and 500 ml. The 300 ml is the retail standard in Europe and Asia: large enough to feel like value, small enough to sit on a shelf next to a shower gel.

This page covers what changes between those sizes, and why the pump — not the bottle — is usually what a customer remembers.

Dose per Stroke Is a Product Decision

A 1.2 ml stroke is right for a face lotion; a 2.0 ml stroke suits a body lotion that people use generously. The bottle is the same 300 ml mould — the pump changes the experience more than the shape does.

If the lotion is thick, a wider dip tube and a larger valve prevent the “pumping air” feeling at the end of the bottle. That is worth specifying before the artwork.

Why 300 ml Feels Premium and 500 ml Feels Economical

At 300 ml the bottle fits one hand; at 500 ml it needs two. That is the whole difference in perceived quality, and it has nothing to do with the plastic.

Wall thickness matters at both sizes: 0.55 mm reads fine on a 300 ml, but on a 500 ml the same wall can flex under a full pump stroke.

PET or HDPE for Lotion

Clear PET shows the emulsion and supports a “clean” positioning; HDPE is opaque, cheaper per gram and hides the fill level as the bottle empties — which is why value lines and professional sizes use it.

If the formula contains a solvent or a high level of essential oil, check the inner compatibility before choosing PET; some oils craze the surface over months.

Decoration That Survives a Bathroom Shelf

A lotion bottle lives in humidity. Paper labels wrinkle; permanent adhesive lifts. Silkscreen and spray-coat finishes are the durable options, and both start at 1,000 pcs (spray coat at 3,000).

For a 300 ml line with three SKUs, the usual setup is the same bottle, three colours and one pump type — cheaper than three different moulds, and easier to keep on shelf together.

Questions We Get on This

What is the standard body lotion bottle size?

300 ml in Europe and Asia; 500 ml for value and family sizes; 200–250 ml for travel and gift sets.

Which pump suits a thick lotion?

A 2.0 ml stroke pump with a wider dip tube. A standard 1.2 ml pump will feel like it is straining.

Can the same bottle be used for lotion and hand soap?

Yes — the 24/410 neck takes both pumps. Only the pump and the label change.

Do you supply the pump with the bottle?

Yes, 24/410 and 28/410 lotion pumps, fitted and tested before packing.
