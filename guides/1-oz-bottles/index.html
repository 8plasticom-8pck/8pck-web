1 Oz Bottles: 30 ml, Samples and the Cost Floor

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

1 oz is 30 ml — the sample and essential-oil size. Below this, the cap and the filling cost more than the bottle, and the price stops falling.

At 30 ml the unit price is dominated by the closure and the filling, not the resin. That is why a 1 oz bottle often costs more than half of a 2 oz.

The Cost Floor

Resin at 30 ml is a few cents. The cap, the label and the filling are not. Below 1 oz the price stops falling because the fixed parts dominate.
