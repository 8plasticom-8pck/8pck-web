250 ml Bottles: the Small Retreat Size, and Why It Fails on a Pump

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

250 ml is the size a brand picks when it wants a lower entry price without looking small. It is also the size where a pump starts to look disproportionate.

250 ml is a bridge size: large enough to be a real product, small enough to sit at an entry price point. It is the most common size in Germany and the Nordics for hand soap and cleanser.

The design tension is the closure. A standard 24/410 pump on a 250 ml body looks top-heavy; a disc-top looks proportionate but doses loosely.

Pump or Disc-Top at 250 ml

A 250 ml with a pump reads as a professional size; with a disc-top it reads as a value size. Both are the same bottle — only the neck fitting changes.

Why 250 ml Wins in Germany and the Nordics

Shelf price points are lower and package sizes smaller than in the US or UK. 250 ml hits the entry price without a small-looking pack.

Where It Fails

A 250 ml at 0.45 mm flexes under a pump stroke, and the pump draws air at the last third. 0.55 mm at the shoulder is the floor for a pump-fitted 250 ml.

Where This Size Fails

A 250 ml pack fails at the interface, not at the mould: the closure thread, the wall at the shoulder, and the label edge in a wet environment.

Thread Mismatch

A 410 closure on a 415 neck threads on and leaks. Confirm the thread code.

Shoulder Flex

Under a full pump stroke a thin shoulder deforms and the pump draws air; 0.55 mm is the floor.

Label Lift

Paper plus humidity equals a lifted edge within weeks. Synthetic or silkscreen.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity (250 ml), neck, closure, quantity, and whether the pack ships filled or empty. Those five answers set the wall, the liner and the carton before a sample is made.

Questions We Get on This

What is a 250 ml bottle used for?

Hand soap, face cleanser, lotion and travel sets; the entry-size retail format in Germany and the Nordics.

What neck does a 250 ml bottle use?

24/410 in almost all cases.

Pump or disc-top?

Pump for dose control, disc-top for cost and thin liquids.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour.

What is the MOQ for a 250 ml bottle?

1,000 pcs per colour on existing moulds.

Which closure fits?

24/410 covers most pumps and caps; 28/410 for heavier pumps at 500 ml and above.

Can it be decorated?

Silkscreen from 1,000 pcs, spray coat and metallising from 3,000.
