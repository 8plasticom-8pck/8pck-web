1 L Bottles: Detergent, Juice and the Wide-Mouth Problem

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

1 L is a household size first and a cosmetic size second: detergent, fabric softener, juice and syrup. The neck widens, the wall thickens, and the closure becomes a pouring device.

At 1 L the pack stops being a cosmetic bottle and becomes a container: pouring, refilling and stacking matter more than shelf presence.

Wide Mouth or Narrow

A 38 mm neck fills faster and pours a thick product; a 28/410 keeps the standard pump and trigger ecosystem. Detergent usually goes 38 mm, juice 28/410 or 38 mm depending on the line.

Material Follows the Product

HDPE for detergent and chemicals; PET for juice and clear liquids, with the caveat that hot fill distorts standard PET.

Handling and Stacking

A full 1 L weighs over a kilogram. The base needs a flat, reinforced design or cases sag in a pallet stack — a common cause of returns.

Where This Size Fails

At 1 L the pack is a container before it is a brand object. Failures come from the shoulder, the base and the closure — in that order.

Shoulder Flex

A full bottle puts load on the shoulder; below 0.65 mm a pump stroke deforms it and draws air.

Base Sag

A flat, reinforced base is needed or cases sag in a pallet stack over weeks.

Closure Mismatch

38 mm and 38-400 are not the same specification; confirm the thread on the drawing.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity (1 L), product type, neck, closure, and whether the pack ships as a retail unit or a refill. Those answers decide resin, wall and base.

Questions We Get on This

What is a 1 L bottle used for?

Detergent, fabric softener, juice, syrup and refill formats.

What neck does a 1 L bottle use?

38 mm for detergent and thick liquids; 28/410 when a standard trigger or pump is needed.

PET or HDPE at 1 L?

HDPE for detergent and chemicals; PET for clear juice, cold fill only.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour; tooling for a new handle or base adds cost.

What is the MOQ for a 1 L bottle?

1,000 pcs per colour on existing moulds.

Can it take a standard pump?

At 1 L usually not — 38 mm necks take screw or measuring caps, not 28/410 pumps.

Is the base reinforced?

Specify it: a full bottle at this size can sag in a pallet stack.
