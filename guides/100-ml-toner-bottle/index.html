100 ml Toner Bottle: What to Specify, and Where It Fails

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

100 ml Toner Bottle in PET: a 20/410 neck with a fine mist sprayer. The mistake to avoid is a sprayer set for water that spits on a viscous toner.

100 ml Toner Bottle is the standard format for toner at this size: PET body, 20/410 neck, fine mist sprayer closure.

Almost every complaint in this combination traces to a sprayer set for water that spits on a viscous toner — a specification decision, not a moulding defect.

Why This Combination

100 ml is the volume toner is normally sold in, and 20/410 is the neck that size is moulded on. The fine mist sprayer follows from the neck, which is why the closure choice is really a neck choice.

The failure we see is a sprayer set for water that spits on a viscous toner.

The Neck Decides the Closure

20/410 takes the fine mist sprayer and the standard range for that neck.

Testing

The closure is fitted and leak-tested on the filled bottle before packing.

Wall, Weight and Shipping

At 100 ml the shoulder carries the load. Below 0.55 mm a pump stroke deforms it and the pump draws air; 0.65 mm where the closure is heavy.

If the pack ships filled, the carton has to support the shoulder — that is where most transit damage starts.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity, product type, neck, closure and quantity. Five lines and we quote with packing data.
