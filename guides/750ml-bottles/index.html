750 ml Bottles: Family Size, 28/410 Neck, and a Wall That Has to Hold

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

750 ml is the family shampoo and pet shampoo size. It is the first size where a 28/410 pump is mandatory and the wall thickness becomes a structural question.

750 ml exists because of price per ml: a family buys once and uses for months. It is also the size where cheap bottles fail, because a full 750 ml puts more load on the shoulder than a 500 ml.

28/410 Is Not Optional

At 750 ml a 24/410 pump looks small and doses too slowly for the volume; 28/410 is the standard, and it needs a wider neck and shoulder.

28/410 Pump

2.0 ml+ per stroke; the default for family shampoo.

Flip-Top

Cheaper, used for refill formats and value lines.

The Wall Has to Hold

A full 750 ml at 0.55 mm flexes under a stroke and the pump draws air. 0.65 mm at the shoulder is the practical floor, and 0.7 mm for a rough handling chain.

Export Economics

A 750 ml carton is heavy: check pallet weight limits before fixing the pack count. Freight per litre drops, but the case can hit a limit a 500 ml case does not.

Where This Size Fails

At 750 ml the pack is a container before it is a brand object. Failures come from the shoulder, the base and the closure — in that order.

Shoulder Flex

A full bottle puts load on the shoulder; below 0.65 mm a pump stroke deforms it and draws air.

Base Sag

A flat, reinforced base is needed or cases sag in a pallet stack over weeks.

Closure Mismatch

38 mm and 38-400 are not the same specification; confirm the thread on the drawing.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity (750 ml), product type, neck, closure, and whether the pack ships as a retail unit or a refill. Those answers decide resin, wall and base.

Questions We Get on This

What is a 750 ml bottle used for?

Family shampoo, conditioner, pet shampoo and refill formats.

What neck does a 750 ml bottle use?

28/410; a 24/410 is too small for the volume.

What wall thickness does 750 ml need?

At least 0.65 mm at the shoulder for a 28/410 pump.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour.

What is the MOQ for a 750 ml bottle?

1,000 pcs per colour on existing moulds.

Can it take a standard pump?

At 1 L usually not — 38 mm necks take screw or measuring caps, not 28/410 pumps.

Is the base reinforced?

Specify it: a full bottle at this size can sag in a pallet stack.
