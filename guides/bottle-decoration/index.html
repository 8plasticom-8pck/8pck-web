Bottle Decoration: Silkscreen, Hot Stamp, Spray Coat — and What Each Does to MOQ

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 2026

Decoration is where a commodity bottle becomes a brand — and where the minimum order quietly triples. Four processes, what each costs in MOQ and lead time, and how to choose between them.

The bottle is the same; the decoration is the difference between a 40-cent pack and a 90-cent pack at retail. It is also the step where buyers discover that four-colour print has a different minimum from one-colour.

Here is what each process actually requires, in numbers.

Silkscreen: the Workhorse

One to four colours, applied directly to the bottle. It is the cheapest way to put a logo on a pack and the most forgiving on small runs — 1,000 pcs, five extra days.

Limitation: it prints on curved surfaces in straight lines. Complex gradients and photographs are not silkscreen work; those go to labels or sleeves.

Hot Stamp and Spray Coat: the Premium Two

Hot stamping presses foil into the surface for a metallic logo. Spray coating covers the whole bottle in matte, gloss or soft-touch. Both need 3,000 pcs because both are set-up-heavy: plates, masking, oven time.

Soft-touch is the finish that most often sells a premium line, and the one that shows fingerprints. Say so to your customer before they approve it.

Labels Are Not a Downgrade

A pressure-sensitive label on a clear bottle can carry full-colour artwork and variable data that silkscreen cannot, at 1,000 pcs and three extra days. For short runs and market tests, it is usually the right first move — then switch to print once the design stops changing.

Artwork: What We Need to Quote

Vector artwork (AI or PDF) with the print area marked, the Pantone references, and the bottle drawing. Without the print area, every supplier quotes a different price for a different amount of ink.

Questions We Get on This

What is the cheapest way to brand a bottle?

One-colour silkscreen at 1,000 pcs. It is also the fastest, adding about five days to production.

Can I mix decoration methods on one order?

Yes, but each method has its own MOQ and its own set-up. A 1,000 pc silkscreen plus 3,000 pc hot stamp means the hot stamp runs at 3,000.

Do you print on the cap as well?

Yes — silkscreen or hot stamp on caps, quoted separately from the bottle.

Can you match a Pantone colour exactly?

We match to the Pantone reference on the bottle material, which shifts slightly on coloured resin. A physical sample is the only reliable approval.
