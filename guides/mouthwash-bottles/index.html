Mouthwash Bottles: the Cap Is the Dose: Moulded Lines at 10 and 20 ml, With a Wide 38 mm Neck

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

Mouthwash packs in 250–500 ml, usually with a 38 mm neck and a measuring cap. The specification question is not the bottle — it is a measuring cap that does not show the dose.

Mouthwash is sold in 250–500 ml, in PET, with a 38 mm neck and a measuring cap. It is used for mouthwash and oral rinse.

Most complaints in this category trace to a measuring cap that does not show the dose. That is a specification decision, made before a mould is cut.

What the Formula Does to the Pack

The cap is the dose: moulded lines at 10 and 20 ml, with a wide 38 mm neck.

The failure we see most in this category is a measuring cap that does not show the dose. It is avoidable at the specification stage: name the formula, the temperature and the closure, and the bottle follows.

Material

PET is the default here; the alternative is only worth it when a different property matters more.

Closure

Measuring Cap on a 38 mm neck is the standard combination at this size.

Sizing and Filling

250–500 ml covers the retail range. Filling speed sets the neck: a wider neck fills faster but needs a bigger cap and a stronger shoulder.

If the pack ships filled, the carton matters as much as the bottle: a shoulder that is not supported arrives cracked.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity, the formula type, the closure you want and the quantity. Four lines are enough to quote with packing data and to catch a measuring cap.

Where This Pack Fails in the Field

The failure that matters for mouthwash and oral rinse is a measuring cap that does not show the dose. It shows up after the first shipment, not on the sample.

Test Before the First Order

Ask for the closure fitted and filled, then leave it on its side for 48 hours.

What to Write in the Spec

Formula type, fill temperature, 38 mm neck and the closure model — those four decide the pack.

What We Do Not Do

Filling, formulation and product-specific registration stay with the brand.

Sizing This Category

250–500 ml is the retail range. For a first order, 1,000 pcs per colour is the practical minimum on an existing mould; a new shape means tooling at about USD 850 and 15–20 days.

Samples take 7 days from the production tool, not from a showroom piece.
