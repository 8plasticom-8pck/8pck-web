Plastic Bottles With Caps: Why Caps Are Quoted Separately

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

A bottle with a cap sounds like one product. In practice they are two moulds, two MOQs and sometimes two suppliers — which is why the price looks split.

Bottles are blow-moulded; caps are injection-moulded. Different machines, different MOQs, and a fit that has to be tested as a pair.

Why They Are Two Products

A blow-moulded bottle and an injection-moulded cap come off different machines. Ordering them together saves handling, not tooling.

Bottle

Blow mould; 1,000 pcs MOQ.

Cap

Injection mould; 1,000 pcs MOQ, separate tool.

What 'Supplied Fitted' Means

We can fit and test the cap on the bottle before packing, which removes one step at the filling line and one source of leaks.

When to Buy Separately

If you already own a cap mould or buy caps locally, specify the neck to match — and send a cap sample.

Questions We Get on This

Is the cap included in the bottle price?

Usually quoted separately; ask for a fitted price.

Can you match my existing cap?

Yes, send a sample; we test it on the bottle.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs for bottles and for caps.

Do you fit the caps?

Yes, fitted and leak-tested before packing.
