20/410 Neck: the Travel and Mist Size

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

20/410 sits between the dropper neck and the standard pump neck: fine mist sprayers, travel flip-tops and 50–100 ml bottles.

20/410 is 20 mm with a 410 thread. It is the size that makes a 50–100 ml bottle look right with a mist sprayer or a disc-top, where an 18 mm would look fragile and a 24 mm too heavy.

What Fits A 20/410

Fine mist sprayers, flip-tops, disc-tops and small pumps. It is the travel and perfume-sample neck.

Fine Mist Sprayer

The classic 20/410 use: perfumes, toners, sanitizers.

Flip or Disc Top

Travel packs and samples where a sprayer is unnecessary.

Why Not 24/410 at This Size

A 24 mm closure on a 50 ml bottle makes the pack look top-heavy and the label area disappears. 20/410 keeps the proportion.

Where It Fails

Mist sprayers set for water spray a toner coarsely. Test the sprayer with the real liquid, not with water.

How to Confirm the Neck Before Ordering

The 20/410 code is a diameter plus a thread profile, and both have to match for travel packs and mist sprayers.

Measure T, E and H

T is thread height, E is the gap between threads, H is the height to the sealing surface.

Send the Closure

A pump that fits a drawing can leak on a bottle. Test the pair, not the part.

Check the Drawing, Not the Photo

Two bottles can look identical and differ by 5 mm of thread.

What to Send in a Brief

Neck (20/410), bottle capacity, closure model and quantity. With those four we confirm fit before quoting.

Questions We Get on This

What is a 20/410 neck used for?

Fine mist sprayers, travel bottles and 50–100 ml cosmetic packs.

What closures fit 20/410?

Mist sprayers, flip-tops, disc-tops and small pumps.

Can I use a 24/410 pump on a 20/410 bottle?

No — different diameter.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour.

What does 20/410 mean?

Diameter in millimetres plus the 410 thread profile — the second number is not a diameter.

Can I mix 410 and 415?

No; they thread together and leak. Confirm the code on the drawing.

Do you test the closure?

Yes, on the filled bottle before packing.
