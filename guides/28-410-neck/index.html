28/410 Neck: for 500–750 ml and Heavier Pumps

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

28/410 is the neck for larger bottles and heavier pumps. It is also where wall thickness stops being a detail and starts being the specification.

28/410 is 28 mm with a 410 thread, used on 500–750 ml bottles where a 24 mm pump would look small and dose too slowly.

Check it before you quote

The free Bottle Spec Checker tests whether your neck fits the closure and whether the wall survives a pump stroke.

When 28/410 Is the Right Choice

Above 500 ml, and whenever the pump is heavy-duty: shampoo, body wash in bulk, pet shampoo, detergent.

750 ml

28/410 is effectively mandatory; 24/410 looks undersized.

500 ml

Either neck works; 28/410 when the pump is large or the product thick.

The Wall Requirement

0.65 mm at the shoulder for a 28/410 pump. Below that the bottle deforms under a stroke and draws air.

Where It Fails

A 28/410 pump on a 28/415 bottle threads on and leaks — the same 410/415 trap as at 24 mm.

How to Confirm the Neck Before Ordering

The 28/410 code is a diameter plus a thread profile, and both have to match for 500–750 ml with heavier pumps.

Measure T, E and H

T is thread height, E is the gap between threads, H is the height to the sealing surface.

Send the Closure

A pump that fits a drawing can leak on a bottle. Test the pair, not the part.

Check the Drawing, Not the Photo

Two bottles can look identical and differ by 5 mm of thread.

What to Send in a Brief

Neck (28/410), bottle capacity, closure model and quantity. With those four we confirm fit before quoting.

Questions We Get on This

What is a 28/410 neck used for?

500–750 ml bottles with heavier pumps: shampoo, body wash, pet shampoo, detergent.

Can I use a 24/410 pump on a 28/410 bottle?

No; different diameter and it will not seal.

What wall thickness does 28/410 need?

At least 0.65 mm at the shoulder.

What is the MOQ?

1,000 pcs per colour.

What does 28/410 mean?

Diameter in millimetres plus the 410 thread profile — the second number is not a diameter.

Can I mix 410 and 415?

No; they thread together and leak. Confirm the code on the drawing.

Do you test the closure?

Yes, on the filled bottle before packing.
