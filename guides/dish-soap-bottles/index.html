Dish Soap Bottles: Dish Soap Is Often Clear and Coloured

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

Dish Soap packs in 500–750 ml, usually with a 28/410 neck and a disc-top. The specification question is not the bottle — it is a disc-top that clogs with a thick gel.

Dish Soap is sold in 500–750 ml, in PET, with a 28/410 neck and a disc-top. It is used for dish soap and washing-up liquid.

Most complaints in this category trace to a disc-top that clogs with a thick gel. That is a specification decision, made before a mould is cut.

Check it before you quote

The free Bottle Spec Checker tests whether your neck fits the closure and whether the wall survives a pump stroke.

What the Formula Does to the Pack

Dish soap is often clear and coloured — PET with a wide disc-top, not a narrow spout.

The failure we see most in this category is a disc-top that clogs with a thick gel. It is avoidable at the specification stage: name the formula, the temperature and the closure, and the bottle follows.

Material

PET is the default here; the alternative is only worth it when a different property matters more.

Closure

Disc-Top on a 28/410 neck is the standard combination at this size.

Sizing and Filling

500–750 ml covers the retail range. Filling speed sets the neck: a wider neck fills faster but needs a bigger cap and a stronger shoulder.

If the pack ships filled, the carton matters as much as the bottle: a shoulder that is not supported arrives cracked.

What to Send in a Brief

Capacity, the formula type, the closure you want and the quantity. Four lines are enough to quote with packing data and to catch a disc-top.

Where This Pack Fails in the Field

The failure that matters for dish soap and washing-up liquid is a disc-top that clogs with a thick gel. It shows up after the first shipment, not on the sample.

Test Before the First Order

Ask for the closure fitted and filled, then leave it on its side for 48 hours.

What to Write in the Spec

Formula type, fill temperature, 28/410 neck and the closure model — those four decide the pack.

What We Do Not Do

Filling, formulation and product-specific registration stay with the brand.

Sizing This Category

500–750 ml is the retail range. For a first order, 1,000 pcs per colour is the practical minimum on an existing mould; a new shape means tooling at about USD 800 and 15–20 days.

Samples take 7 days from the production tool, not from a showroom piece.
