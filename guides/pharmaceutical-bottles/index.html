Pharmaceutical Bottles: the Container, Not the Medicine

By Joe Joo · 8PCK · Shantou · Updated 26 October 2026

A pharmaceutical bottle is HDPE with a child-resistant closure and a documented specification. We supply the container; the licence holder qualifies the product.

Pharma packaging is a documentation exercise as much as a moulding one: the bottle, the closure and the liner have to be specified and held consistent batch to batch.

Check it before you quote

The free Bottle Spec Checker tests whether your neck fits the closure and whether the wall survives a pump stroke.

What We Supply and What We Do Not

We mould and supply the container: HDPE bottles, child-resistant closures, induction liners. We do not fill, and we do not issue pharmacopoeia compliance for the finished product.

Container

Bottle, cap, liner — with dimensional specification.

Documents

Resin declaration, dimensional report; the drug qualification stays with the licence holder.

Child-Resistant Closures

A push-and-turn CR cap is the most expensive part on a small pharma bottle, and it has to be tested on the specific bottle rather than supplied loose.

Batch Consistency

Pharma buyers care about dimensional consistency more than colour. Keep the mould, the resin grade and the process window fixed, and say so in writing.

Questions We Get on This

Can you supply pharmaceutical bottles?

Yes, HDPE containers with CR caps and liners; the drug qualification is the licence holder's responsibility.

Do you supply child-resistant caps?

Yes, tested on the bottle.

What sizes?
