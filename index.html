Who we supply

Who We Supply

Most of our work is repeat orders on stock moulds; the occasional new shape is the exception.

Distributors

Packaging distributors in the US, UK, Germany, France, Australia and the Gulf who keep stock and need a factory that can repeat a colour exactly. We quote per SKU with packing data, so the freight calculation is yours, not a guess.

Brand Owners

Independent skincare, haircare and personal-care brands ordering 1,000–20,000 pcs. One mould, one colour, one label — usually with a silkscreen or a spray coat that makes a stock bottle read as bespoke.

Contract Manufacturers

Fillers who buy for several brands at once and need the same bottle in three colours, delivered on a schedule that matches their line. Mixed containers are normal for us.

What We Do Not Do

We do not fill, formulate, design labels, or handle hot-fill above 60 °C in PET. We do not claim FDA, REACH or GRS certificates we do not hold.

Saying this up front saves a sample round: if your product needs a heat-set bottle or a fill line we would have to subcontract, we will tell you before you send a deposit.

Buyer questions

What Buyers Ask Before the First Order

Short answers. The long versions are in the guides.
What is the minimum order quantity?

1,000 pcs per colour on an existing mould. Below that we quote stock colours from inventory.

Is there a tooling cost?

No on the 3,000+ moulds CXWN already holds. A new blow mould starts at USD 800 and takes 15–20 days.

How long does production take?

25–35 days after sample approval for stock colours; custom colours and heavy decoration add 5–7 days.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes — samples from existing moulds are free, courier collect, and leave within 7 days.

Who issues the contract and invoice?

The factory: Shantou Chuangxin Weinuo Plastic Packaging Products Co., Ltd. (CXWN). 8PCK is the English export desk; payment goes to CXWN.

Which compliance documents do you hold?

ISO 9001:2015 (PCC85724Q01277R1S), RoHS reports for PE and PET, and an EU 10/2011 + 1935/2004 migration test on PE grades. Scans are on the quality page.

Can you match a specific colour?

Yes, to a Pantone reference. Colour matching is quoted per colour and the 1,000 pcs MOQ applies per colour.

Which markets do you ship to?

40+ countries, mainly the US, EU, Southeast Asia and the Middle East. Terms are EXW, FOB or CIF.
