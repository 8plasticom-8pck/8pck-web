About

One Factory, One Contact, No Layers

You write to the person who quotes. There is no account manager between you and the floor.

Founded in 2011 in Haojiang

The factory started with injection moulding for caps, added blow moulding for bottles, then extrusion for tubes. Today 20 lines run across 7,142 m², with the mould shop and QC lab in the same building.

Most of our customers are distributors and brand owners in Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. The work is repeat orders on stock moulds, plus the occasional new shape.

Who answers your email

Joe Joo

I run 8PCK from Shantou, next door to the factory. Every inquiry comes to my inbox and I write the quotation myself — the price you get is the price the factory will run, not a marketing number that gets renegotiated later.

If a spec will not work — a wall too thin for a pump, a hot fill in PET, a mould that would cost more than the order — I would rather say so before you place it. That is the whole reason this site lists documents we actually hold instead of a wall of badges.
