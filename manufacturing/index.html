The Short Version

Injection for anything with a thread, a seal groove or a tolerance. Injection stretch blow when the neck must be precise and the body must be strong and light. Extrusion blow for a handle, a wide mouth or a cheap opaque body. Decoration last, because it carries its own MOQ.
