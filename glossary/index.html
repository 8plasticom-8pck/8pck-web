Glossary

Plastic Packaging Terms, in Plain English

The words buyers and suppliers use differently — and what each one actually means on a drawing.

Neck Finish

The opening specification, written as diameter/thread — e.g. 24/410 is 24 mm diameter with a 410 thread profile. Read more →

MOQ

Minimum order quantity. At 8PCK it is 1,000 pcs per colour on existing moulds. Read more →

Blow Mould

The tool that forms a bottle. About USD 800 and 15–20 days at CXWN, and it belongs to the customer. Read more →

Preform

The test-tube-shaped PET piece that is stretch-blown into a bottle. Read more →

Airless

A pack with a piston and no dip tube, so the product never meets air. Read more →

Dropper

A pipette closure on an 18/410 neck, used for serums and oils. Read more →

Lotion Pump

A pump that dispenses 1.2–2.0 ml per stroke; the default closure for 250–500 ml bottles. Read more →

Foam Pump

A pump that mixes air with a low-viscosity formula at the nozzle to produce foam. Read more →

Trigger Sprayer

A hand-squeezed sprayer on a 28/410 neck, used for home care and garden. Read more →

Disc-Top Cap

A cap that opens on a hinge with a small orifice; the cheapest controlled-flow closure. Read more →

Induction Seal

A foil liner sealed to a jar rim by heat; it needs a flat land on the rim. Read more →

EPE Liner

A foam liner inside a screw cap. Cheap and fine for dry goods and creams; swells with oils. Read more →

Sleeve

A shrink film wrapped around a bottle. Makes PET harder to recycle. Read more →

Silkscreen

Direct printing on the bottle; from 1,000 pcs, and the most durable decoration for a wet environment. Read more →

Spray Coat

A painted finish on a bottle; from 3,000 pcs and adds 5–8 days. Read more →

Hot Stamp

Foil stamping for a metallic look; from 3,000 pcs. Read more →

PET

Polyethylene terephthalate — clear, stiff, widely recycled; poor with strong solvents. Read more →

HDPE

High-density polyethylene — opaque, tough, cheap, good with surfactants. Read more →

LDPE

Low-density polyethylene — soft and squeezable, used for sauce and gel bottles. Read more →

PETG

A thicker-walled clear plastic that reads closer to glass than PET. Read more →

PP

Polypropylene — the material for hot fill above 60 °C and for most closures. Read more →

Mass Balance

An accounting method for recycled content; not the same as physically recycled content. Read more →

AQL

Acceptable quality limit — the sampling standard used at inspection. Read more →

Incoterms

Delivery terms. 8PCK quotes FOB Shantou by default; EXW and CIF on request. Read more →
