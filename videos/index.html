What the Clips Show — and Why We Publish Them

A buyer ordering 20,000 bottles from a supplier 9,000 km away is being asked to trust a drawing and an email. Video does not replace a factory audit, but it removes the most common doubt: whether the plant exists and whether the line that will run your mould is real.

Three of the clips are technical rather than promotional. The wall-thickness and neck check clip shows how a batch is measured; the leak-test clip shows a fitted pump being tested on the bottle; the packing clip shows how bottles are cartoned for a sea container. If you are comparing suppliers, these are the three processes to ask about.

How to Use These Clips When You Are Choosing a Supplier

Watch the moulding clip and count the cavities in the tool. A 4-cavity tool makes 4 bottles a cycle — that number sets the price and the lead time more than anything else in the quote.

Then watch the QC clip. Any factory can show a machine running; the difference is whether anyone is measuring. If a supplier cannot show you a neck gauge or a leak test, the certificate is the only evidence you will get.

Frequently Asked

Are these videos from your own factory?

No. 8PCK is the export desk; the plant is CXWN in Shantou, founded in 2011, and that is where every clip was filmed.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes. Buyers are welcome at CXWN in Haojiang, Shantou; we arrange the visit and interpret. A video call walk-through is also possible if travel is not practical.

Do you have videos of a specific bottle?

Ask for the model number and we will film the mould running. For stock moulds this is usually possible within a week.

Which video should I watch first?

The QC clip, if you are comparing suppliers. The moulding clip, if you want to understand why tooling and cavity count drive the price.
