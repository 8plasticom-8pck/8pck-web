LDPE vs HDPE: Which Squeeze Bottle

LDPE is the soft-wall squeeze: it collapses as product leaves, so it dispenses to the last drop. HDPE is firmer and springs back, keeping the bottle's shape on a shelf. Both are blow-moulded, both start at 1,000 pcs.

How to Choose in Practice

Squeeze a filled sample: if the bottle must return to shape, HDPE. If it should collapse so the product keeps flowing to the top, LDPE.

Where LDPE Fails

Greasy products migrate through thin LDPE walls over months — the bottle looks oily on the outside. For oils and solvent-based products, HDPE or a barrier layer is the safer choice.

Compatibility, Packing and Tolerance

Closures That Fit

Send the neck finish and we match a closure from the same plant: disc-top and flip-top caps, lotion and foam pumps, mist sprayers and droppers. Closures are fitted and tested on the bottle before packing, which is where most leaks originate.

Packing and Freight

Carton quantity, carton dimensions and gross weight are quoted with the unit price. FOB Shantou is the default term; EXW and CIF are quoted on request. Payment is 30 % deposit, 70 % against the bill of lading.

Tolerance

Dimensions hold to ± 1.5 % and weight to ± 1 g against the approved drawing. Anything outside that range is remade, not discounted.

Decoration Options and What They Cost in MOQ

Silkscreen in one to four colours starts at 1,000 pcs and adds about five days; the plate is a one-off, which is why it does not repeat on a reorder. Labels are cheaper for multi-colour artwork and allow short runs. Hot stamp, spray coat and metallising start at 3,000 pcs because the line set-up is heavier and the reject rate is higher.

Decoration also decides the recycling story. A paper label with a wash-off adhesive keeps a PET bottle in the PET stream; a full-body sleeve does not. If the pack carries a recyclability claim, the decoration is part of the claim, not a design detail.

Sampling and Approval

A sample from an existing mould takes 7 days and comes from the production tool, not a showroom piece. For a new shape, tooling takes 15–20 days before sampling. We ship the sample with the drawing it was made to, so the approval is against a number rather than a photograph.

If the formula contains alcohol, a high level of essential oil or a particulate, say so at sample stage — the compatibility test runs four weeks and it is cheaper to find a problem then than in a container.

How We Quote and Ship

Send capacity, neck, quantity and the closure you intend to use. We reply within 24 hours with unit price, packing data (pcs per carton, carton size, gross weight) and lead time — the packing data is what your freight forwarder needs, so it is quoted with the price rather than after.

Production is 25–35 days after sample approval on existing moulds, 15–20 days longer if a new mould is needed. Payment is 30 % deposit and 70 % against the bill of lading; FOB Shantou is the default term, EXW and CIF on request.

What We Do Not Do

We do not fill, formulate or design labels, and we do not hot-fill in standard PET above about 60 °C. We hold ISO 9001:2015 (PCC85724Q01277R1S), RoHS reports for PE and PET and an EU 10/2011 migration test — and we do not claim FDA, REACH or GRS certification we do not hold.

Frequently Asked

Is LDPE food safe?

Food-grade LDPE is, with the resin declaration. Ask for it per batch.

Which is cheaper?

They are close; the wall thickness and closure usually decide the price.

Can LDPE bottles be recycled?

Yes, as LDPE film and rigid are collected in some schemes; HDPE is more widely accepted.

What neck do squeeze bottles use?

24/410 for cosmetics, 38 mm for sauces and thick products.
